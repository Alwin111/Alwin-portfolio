About Me

Background, technical interests, and the work that drives me.

I'm Alwin Varghese, an Electronics and Computer Engineering student at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amritapuri and Wireless Security Lead at bi0s Hardware — India's top hardware security student group.

My work spans the full hardware stack: PCB design, embedded firmware, protocol-level wireless security research, and CTF challenge creation. I lead research into vulnerabilities across BLE, Wi-Fi, and proprietary RF protocols.

I'm drawn to the intersection of hardware and security — the space where physical systems meet digital protocols, and where vulnerabilities can have real-world consequences. My projects are built to understand, test, and strengthen that boundary.

Embedded Systems

Bare-metal and RTOS firmware for microcontrollers. From register-level programming to full application stacks.

Wireless Security

BLE, Wi-Fi, and RF protocol analysis. Vulnerability research, exploitation, and defensive countermeasure design.

Multi-layer board layout, schematics, and custom hardware prototyping from concept to fabrication.
